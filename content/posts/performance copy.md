## Introduction

My name is Dave (if you didn't guess that already from the domain) and recently I have been traveling a lot by train with my bike

### Rinko culture

In Japan, there is a culture called [Rinko][^rinko-wiki] where people take their bikes on the train. It's a great way to explore the country and see things that you wouldn't be able to see otherwise.

### European trains

So in Germany, there is a good high speed train network, and on a lot of the trains they have a special carriage where you can take your bike. It costs 

> You always need a long-distance bicycle ticket and a bicycle space reservation on long-distance trains. You can only take your bicycle with you if you book both in advance.

But that poses a problem in the case of:

- Your route is served by the newer ICE neo 4, which as fast as it might be has no room for bikes
- You want to take a popular route in the middle of summer when all the prepared (love you people) Germans have booked all the bike reservation slots long in advance.
